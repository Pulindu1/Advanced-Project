\section{Results and Evaluation}
\label{sec:results}


\subsection{Evaluation of the Evaluation Method Adopted}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Triangulation of Data}: Use of mixed-methods combining platform logs (Time-to-Flag), player surveys, and automated validation to ensure data validity.
\item \textbf{Metric Selection}: Defense of Normalized Gain ⟨g⟩ as the gold standard for measuring technical knowledge transfer.
\item \textbf{Exploit-as-a-Test}: Using Python/Bash exploit scripts to establish an objective "ground truth" for solvability, independent of student skill.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Clarity of the Results}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Technical Success Matrix}: A tabular breakdown of completion status and functional verification for all 8--9 challenges.
\item \textbf{Automated Verification Outcomes}: Results from Jest/Supertest (Basic) and custom exploit scripts (Advanced) proving technical adequacy.
\item \textbf{Pilot Performance Data}: Visualization of student solve times and failure rates across difficulty tiers to assess challenge calibration.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Suitability of Approach}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Pedagogical Accessibility}: Evaluation of "bread-crumbing" and progressive scaffolding in maintaining a cognitive "flow" state.
\item \textbf{Anti-Cheating Integrity}: Analysis of how per-user HMAC-SHA256 flag generation effectively mitigated peer-to-peer flag sharing.
\item \textbf{Technology Alignment}: Appraisal of modern stacks (React, Node.js, Laravel) in providing a superior realistic experience over legacy PHP.
\end{itemize}
