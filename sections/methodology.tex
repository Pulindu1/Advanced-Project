\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:methodology}
 
\subsection{Design Philosophy and Guiding Principles}
\label{sec:design-philosophy}
 
The Related Work identified several gaps in the current landscape of
CTF-based cybersecurity education: a narrow focus on classical
vulnerability classes with modern exploit categories rarely
represented~\cite{Sasiwanit}, limited empirical evidence for
measurable learning gain within graded web-specific
coursework~\cite{Meinsma2022}, unresolved challenges around secure
distribution of containerised challenges with embedded
secrets~\cite{raj2016}, and a nascent understanding of how LLM-based
solvers interact with multi-stage exploitation
tasks~\cite{ji2025}. This section describes the methodology by which
these gaps were translated into a concrete suite of web
exploitation CTF challenges, beginning with the design philosophy that
governed every subsequent architectural and pedagogical decision. The
remainder of the Methodology then details the challenge
implementations (Section~\ref{sec:challenge-design}), the
academic integrity infrastructure
(Section~\ref{sec:flag-personalisation}), the containerisation
strategy (Section~\ref{sec:containerisation}), and the
verification, validation, and testing methodology
(Section~\ref{sec:vvt}) whose results are presented in
Section~\ref{sec:results}.
 
Five core principles were established at the outset of the project and
applied consistently across all challenges. Each principle
addresses a specific finding from the literature and imposes a
measurable constraint on the design. Table~\ref{tab:design-principles}
summarises the mapping between principles and their motivating sources;
the paragraphs that follow elaborate on each in turn.
 
\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Design Principles and Their Literature Foundations}
\label{tab:design-principles}
\begin{tabular}{|p{2.8cm}|p{5cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Principle} & \textbf{Motivating Literature} \\ \hline
Realism over artificiality &
Fuzyll~\cite{Fuzyll2015}: learning as the path to the flag;
Vykopal et al.~\cite{Vykopal}: graded CTFs risk
``flag hunting'' when scenarios lack authenticity \\ \hline
Progressive within-domain scaffolding &
Cs\'{i}kszentmih\'{a}lyi~\cite{Csikszentmihalyi}: Flow Theory
(challenge--skill balance);
Balaji et al.~\cite{Balaji2025}: sequential gating in multi-level CTFs \\ \hline
Broad OWASP coverage including under-represented classes &
Sasiwanit et al.~\cite{Sasiwanit}: existing platforms limited
to classical vulnerabilities;
OWASP Top~10~\cite{OWASP2025} as the coverage framework \\ \hline
Per-user personalisation for academic integrity &
Chothia \& Novakovic (SecGen)~\cite{chothia2023}: personalised
lab environments;
Vykopal et al.~\cite{Vykopal}: plagiarism as a structural risk \\ \hline
Technology stack diversity &
Novel contribution (no single surveyed platform varies its
implementation stack per challenge). \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
