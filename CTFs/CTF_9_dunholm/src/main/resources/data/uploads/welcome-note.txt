Welcome note (draft), Phase 2 open-label extension, DR-2024-017
Author: Sophie Chen, Trial Coordinator

Dear participant,

Thank you for taking part in the open-label extension of the NIMMOD-2
study. This note is a short reminder of what happens at each of your
visits over the next six months.

Visit structure

Each visit follows the same pattern. You will be seen by a member of
the clinical team, the nurse team will take the blood samples that are
part of the biomarker panel, and the study coordinator (that is me)
will check that your diary is up to date and answer any questions you
have. Visits should take no more than two hours.

Your study diary

The study diary is your record of what you notice between visits. Any
small change that is new or different is worth noting, even if you are
not sure whether it is related to the study drug. The clinical team
uses these notes when we review your visit.

Contacting the team

If you need to reach the team between visits, call the study line on
the number on your contact card. Out of hours, the answering service
takes a short message and the on-call clinician returns the call.

Final note

This is a draft. The final welcome pack will include the MHRA
participant information sheet and the updated contact card. The draft
will be reviewed by James and Rachel before it goes out to participants.

-- Sophie Chen
Trial Coordinator
