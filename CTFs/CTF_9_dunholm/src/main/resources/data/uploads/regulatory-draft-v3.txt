Dunholm Research Ltd
MHRA submission draft v3, DR-2024-017 (NIMMOD-2)
Prepared by: Dr. Helen Cross, Research Lead
Status: internal draft, not for external circulation

1. Study rationale

NIMMOD-2 extends the DR-2023-011 baseline survey into an open-label Phase 2
dose-ranging study evaluating neuroinflammation modulation in participants
with early-onset multiple sclerosis. The baseline survey established the
relevant biomarker panel and confirmed that the expected safety margins
held at the proposed therapeutic dose range.

2. Endpoints

Primary: change from baseline in the NIMMOD composite biomarker score at
week 24. Secondary: standard MS functional measures (EDSS, T25FW, 9HPT).

3. Statistical plan

Intent-to-treat population, mixed-model repeated measures, pre-specified
subgroup analyses by age at onset and baseline EDSS. Analyses will be led
by the Newcastle collaborator site.

4. Safety monitoring

DSMB convened quarterly. Stopping rules per MHRA guidance; the blinded
monitoring team has standing access to the raw biomarker panels.

5. Regulatory history

Pre-submission meeting with MHRA held 2024-03-11. Feedback was positive;
the MHRA requested clarification on the biomarker validation path which is
addressed in Appendix B.

6. Open items before external circulation

The redaction review on Appendix C (site monitoring plan) has not yet been
completed. Do not circulate externally until Rachel signs off.

End of draft v3.
