Dunholm Research Ltd
Board Minutes, September 2024
Chair: Dr. Helen Cross
Attendees: Helen Cross (chair), Amir Patel, Rachel Osei, Dr. James Whitfield,
           Sophie Chen (minute taker)
Apologies: none

1. Open

The chair opened the meeting at 09:30 in the Elvet meeting room at Durham
Science Park. The previous minutes were confirmed without amendment.

2. Competitor disclosure

Helen summarised the event: on 2024-09-08 a competitor published a
pre-print whose methods section includes three paragraphs of the NIMMOD-2
Phase 2 dossier verbatim, including the unredacted biomarker panel names
and the draft sponsor response. No pre-publication sharing with the
competitor was authorised.

Rachel reported that she is leading the internal investigation. The
security team is reviewing TrialVault access logs across the relevant
period. The immediate concerns are:

  a) How the draft content left the TrialVault boundary.
  b) Whether any other embargoed material is at risk.
  c) Whether disclosure to the MHRA is required.

James confirmed that the Phase 2 protocol has not been altered as a result
of the disclosure and that enrolment can continue under the existing
protocol.

3. Technical response

Amir reported that the TrialVault application has been in production for
eighteen months and has been well maintained. He noted that there is no
evidence of an external intrusion and that the logging subsystem is
functioning correctly.

Rachel requested that the access-log review be completed by the security
team rather than by the CTO function, given the need for independence.
The board agreed.

4. External advice

The board agreed to commission an external audit of TrialVault, scoped to
access control, cryptographic handling, and log integrity. Helen will
draft the terms of reference.

5. Communications

Draft communications to the sponsor and the MHRA are being prepared by
Helen; no external statement will be issued until the audit is complete.

6. Close

Next meeting: October 2024.
