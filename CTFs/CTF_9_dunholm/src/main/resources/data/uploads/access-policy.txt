Dunholm Research Ltd, Information Access Policy (short form)

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers access to all Dunholm Research systems and documents,
including the TrialVault application, the archived regulatory submissions,
the participant welcome materials, and any data classified as RESTRICTED
or INTERNAL.

2. Named access

Access to RESTRICTED documents is granted by the Research Lead (Helen
Cross) and reviewed quarterly. Access to INTERNAL documents is granted
on appointment by the Security Lead (Rachel Osei). Access to audit
engagements is explicitly delegated in writing.

3. Authentication

Staff authenticate to TrialVault using their Dunholm username and
password. Password reuse with external systems is not permitted.
Multi-factor authentication is not yet enabled and is a standing item on
the security roadmap.

4. Handover handling

During the current handover and external audit, external auditor
accounts are created with the standard username pattern (four letters
plus two digits) and are given researcher-level access. These accounts
are recorded in the audit register and removed at the close of the
engagement.

5. Acceptable use

All access is recorded in the TrialVault logs. Personal use of the
system is not permitted. Copying RESTRICTED content out of TrialVault
requires written authorisation from the Research Lead.

6. Reporting concerns

Concerns about access, configuration, or possible disclosure should be
reported to the Security Lead.
