Dunholm Research Ltd, Staff Handbook (extract)

A letter from the managing director

Welcome to Dunholm Research. We are a small clinical research company
based at Durham Science Park. Our work is focused on early-stage trial
design in neuroinflammation, cardiorenal safety, and oncology biomarker
screening. The company was founded in 2019 and has been profitable since
the end of 2022.

Our core platform, TrialVault, is an internal editorial and document
management system that supports the full life cycle of our studies, from
protocol drafting through to regulatory submission. The integrity of
TrialVault is a direct expression of our integrity as a company, and
everyone who works here is responsible for maintaining it.

-- Dr. Helen Cross, Managing Director

Leadership

Dr. Helen Cross, Managing Director. Joined Dunholm 2019. Previously at
Northumbria Biomedical. Responsible for clinical strategy and the
sponsor relationship.

Amir Patel, Chief Technology Officer. Joined Dunholm 2021. Previously at
two clinical trial platform vendors. Responsible for TrialVault,
infrastructure, and the technical side of the regulatory response.

Rachel Osei, Security Lead. Joined Dunholm 2023. Previously in the
information-security function at a London teaching hospital trust.
Responsible for security policy, internal review, and the audit liaison.

Dr. James Whitfield, Clinical Lead. Joined Dunholm 2020. Principal
investigator on DR-2024-017 and the clinical contact for the DSMB.

Sophie Chen, Trial Coordinator. Joined Dunholm 2023. Site liaison for
the three open studies and the point of contact for participant
communications.

Offices

We are based at a single office at Durham Science Park. The post room
handles outbound regulatory correspondence weekly. Visitors should sign
in at reception on Meadowfield Avenue.

Security posture

All staff have read and acknowledged the Information Access Policy. The
short-form version of that policy is circulated at induction and
reviewed annually.
