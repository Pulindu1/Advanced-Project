About PressRoom

PressRoom is the Greystone Gazette's in-house editorial system. It holds drafts, editorial notes, and publishing records for everything the paper has run since the 2023 digital switch-over. PressRoom was built by Marcus Webb in 2022. It is not connected to the print archive.

If you have forgotten your password, email editorial@greystonegazette.example and Sarah will reset it by hand. There is no automated recovery route.
